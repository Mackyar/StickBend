# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (first release)

- Map left and right stick axes to pitch bend, any MIDI CC, or off.
- Keyboard MIDI passes through unchanged.
- Deadzone, MIDI channel and controller slot settings.
- Four Stick parameters for linking to other plugins and for automation. Recorded Stick envelopes play back as bends and CCs while the stick rests.
- Live controller visualizer.
- VST3 and CLAP for Windows.
