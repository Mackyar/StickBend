# StickBend Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

StickBend is an audio plugin made by Macky Ar (Mahesh Arora). This policy explains what the plugin does with your information. The short version: **nothing leaves your computer.**

## What StickBend collects

Nothing. StickBend has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no advertising and no licence activation.

## Internet access

StickBend never connects to the internet by itself. The only time anything goes online is when you click one of the links in the About panel. That opens the page in your own web browser, and from then on that website's own privacy policy applies.

## Game controller input

StickBend reads your controller's sticks, triggers and buttons through Windows' built-in XInput system, many times per second. These readings are turned into MIDI inside the plugin straight away. They are never stored, logged or sent anywhere.

## Your settings

Your settings (stick mappings, CC numbers, MIDI channel, deadzone and controller slot) are saved by your DAW inside your project file, the same as any other plugin. StickBend does not write any other files to your computer.

## Children

StickBend collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If a future version changes any of this, this policy will be updated and the date above will change. Any version that starts collecting data would say so clearly before release.
